Connect your controller

Plug the controller into this computer or pair it over Bluetooth, then press a button. It should appear automatically.

For PlayStation firmware, hardware identifiers, touch, motion and battery, use a USB cable and select Connect PlayStation in a browser with WebHID support, such as Chrome or Edge.

After connection, choose Full test, Live inputs or Controller info. Stick precision is included in Live inputs.

ssfps5tester

Beta v0.7. Controller tests and information stay in this browser. No analytics are used.

PlayStation USB decoding and metadata queries are adapted from DualShock Tools by the_al and contributors, under the MIT license.

Input observations do not certify hardware health. Firmware, calibration and pairing settings are not changed. Microphone audio is metered locally and is not recorded or uploaded.

The PS5 panel uses the separate Chiaki-ng application for Remote Play (AGPL-3.0). Pairing and account details remain in that application.
